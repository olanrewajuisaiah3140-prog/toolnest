Quick questions

Do I need to sign up?

No. These tools do not require an account.

Are uploaded files stored?

ToolNest's PDF and image processing is designed to handle uploads in memory rather than saving them as permanent files. Hosting infrastructure may still process logs and temporary data; see our Privacy Policy.

Is PDF compression always smaller?

No. Some PDFs are already optimized and may not shrink further.
