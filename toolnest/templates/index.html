GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know before you start.

Do I need to create an account?＋

No. ToolNest is free to use and requires no registration.

What happens to files I upload?＋

Files are processed for your request. Some tools use temporary server files, which are removed after processing. Avoid uploading sensitive documents; see our Privacy Policy.

Can I use ToolNest on my phone?＋

Yes. ToolNest is designed for phones, tablets and desktops. Some large files may be limited by free server resources.

Will PDF compression always reduce size?＋

Not necessarily. Already-optimized PDFs may remain the same size. Stronger settings can reduce image quality, so review the result.
